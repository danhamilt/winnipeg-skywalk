🚶 Directions from the Radisson

Via Skywalk (Recommended)

  • 1. Enter Skywalk from Radisson (7am–6pm weekdays)
  • 2. Walk west to Canada Life Centre
  • 3. South through Cityplace → Winnipeg Square
  • 4. East on Portage Ave → south on Donald St
  • 5. Arrive at 185 Donald — ~10–15 min

On Foot (Street Level)

  • 1. Exit Radisson onto Portage Ave
  • 2. Walk east ~300m, turn south on Donald
  • 3. Continue south to 185 Donald — ~15–20 min
