Directions

Directions from the Radisson Hotel

🚶 Via Skywalk (Recommended)

  • 1. Enter Skywalk from Radisson lobby (7am–6pm weekdays)
  • 2. Walk west toward Canada Life Centre
  • 3. Continue south through Cityplace toward Winnipeg Square
  • 4. East on Portage Ave, then south on Donald Street
  • 5. Arrive at 185 Donald Street — ~10–15 min

🚶 On Foot (Street Level)

  • 1. Exit Radisson onto Portage Avenue
  • 2. Walk east ~300m, turn south on Donald Street
  • 3. Continue south to 185 Donald — ~15–20 min
