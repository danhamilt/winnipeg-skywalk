Entertainment & Sports Hub

Canada Life Centre

canadalifecentre.ca

Home arena of the Winnipeg Jets (NHL) and Manitoba Moose (AHL), hosting stadium concerts, family shows, and major sporting events.

Jets Gear Store (True North Shop)

Primary Flagship Location

300 Portage Avenue at the corner of Portage & Hargrave (street level with direct internal access to the main atrium and skywalk elevators).

Merchandise Offerings

  • Apparel: Official home, away, and third jerseys, practice jerseys, headwear, youth apparel, and exclusive streetwear.
  • Customization Services: On-site heat-press and sewn jersey lettering/numbering (custom nameplates or current players).
  • Authentic Game-Used Gear: Game-issued/worn jerseys, broken sticks, game-used pucks, and locker-room equipment.

Game-Day Skywalk Outposts

Multiple specialized Jets Gear Kiosks are set up on event nights throughout the venue, including the Skywalk Atrium, Main Concourse (North End), and Upper Concourse.

Shop True North Online

Ticket Box Office & Will Call

Location: Corner of Portage Ave & Donald St inside the main atrium at Gate 1. Access via main atrium escalators or glass elevators down from Gate 6 skywalk level.

  • Services: Ticketmaster sales, will call pickup (physical collection/photo-ID), and accessible seating requests.
  • Hours: Weekdays 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM. On event days, remains open through the start of the event.

Dining & Drinks

  • Moxies

    Multi-level restaurant and vibrant cocktail bar with direct indoor access right off the Portage Avenue entrance.

  • Event Bars & Premium Lounges

    Crown Royal Club, private suites, and craft beer kiosks open exclusively on event days on the inner concourses.

Indoor Parkades & Skybridge Connections

The arena is wrapped by a network of elevated climate-controlled skybridges, allowing you to park in a heated garage and walk directly to your seats without ever going outdoors.

Direct Connected Skybridges

  • Gate 6 (Cityplace Skywalk): Spans south across Graham Avenue into Cityplace. Features event-day security screening (X-ray/ticket scanners) for indoor entry.
  • True North Square Bridge: Spans southwest toward Carlton St & Hargrave St, linking directly to Hargrave St. Market.
  • Donald Street Bridge: Spans east across Donald St, connecting the atrium toward the Radisson Hotel.

Top Heated Parkades (Direct Access)

  • True North Square Parkade: (242 Hargrave) Connect via Hargrave Skybridge.
  • Cityplace Parkade (Lot 1): Connect via Gate 6.
  • Millennium Library Parkade: Link through the Cityplace south extension.
  • Portage Place Parkade: Enter via the North Portage skybridge.
  • Radisson Hotel Garage: Connect via Donald Street skybridge.
